ABOUT ROOTS RUN DEEP

Reading support that is explicit, encouraging, and built around the learner.

Roots Run Deep Literacy provides one-to-one reading intervention for students who need more direct, systematic support with foundational reading and spelling skills.

Lessons are carefully paced, interactive, and designed to help students understand how words work—not simply memorize them.
